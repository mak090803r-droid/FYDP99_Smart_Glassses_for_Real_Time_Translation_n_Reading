ZH16-02-小型无人船的河道测绘练习 某高校测绘社团在一段封闭管理的人工河道内使用小型无人船进行训练。 船体搭载卫星定位模块、测深传感器和低速无线链路，任务是沿预先规划 的航线记录水深变化，而不是进行正式水文调查。训练区长约420米，水 面较平静，两端都设有岸边观察员。下水前，操作员检查螺旋桨、天线和 电池固定带，并确认手动接管开关能够立即停止自动航行。 第一条航线由十六个航点组成，设定速度为每秒1.2米。无人船在开阔水 面上能够稳定跟踪路径，但靠近一处混凝土弯道时出现连续偏移。观察员 没有直接修改测深数据，而是先查看定位状态和船体姿态记录。结果发 现，弯道附近高墙使卫星信号质量下降，同时侧风把船头推向外侧。第二 次航行降低了该区域的速度，并把两个航点向河道中央移动约3米，路径 误差明显减小。 异常点处理 测深数据也需要质量检查。传感器在水草密集位置偶尔给出突然变浅的读 数，与前后点变化不一致。社团成员在地图上保留这些原始点，并用标记 注明“疑似水草反射”，没有直接删除。随后他们从岸边使用带刻度测杆 抽查了三个位置，确认其中两个异常点并不代表真实河床起伏。这个过程 让学生理解，自动采集的数据仍然需要结合环境和独立检查来解释。 训练结束后，所有航迹、深度记录和操作日志按日期归档。报告中特别区 分了“传感器读数”“经现场核查的读数”和“尚未确认的异常值”。社 团计划下一次加入更严格的重复航线测试，以比较同一路径在不同风速下 的差异。此次练习没有追求漂亮的三维图，而是把重点放在安全接管、异 常保留和可追溯的数据处理上。

  TRANSLATED: ZH16-02- A small unmanned riverboat mapping exercise for a university mapping society using a small unmanned boat for training in a closed managed artificial river. The vessels are equipped with satellite positioning modules, depth sensors and low-speed wireless links, and are tasked with recording depth changes along pre-planned routes, rather than conducting formal hydrological surveys. The training area is about 420 meters long, the water is calm, and there are shore observers at both ends. Before the dive, the operator checks the propeller, antenna and battery fastener and confirms that the manual take-off switch can stop automatically immediately. The first route consists of sixteen runways with a speed of 1.2 m/s. The drones are able to steadily track the path on open water, but there is a continuous deflection when approaching a concrete tunnel. The observer does not directly modify the depth measurement data, but first checks the position and body posture records. The results showed that the high walls near the tunnel reduced the quality of the satellite signal, while the side winds pushed the boat's bow outwards. The second voyage slowed down the area and moved the two points of navigation about 3 meters to the center of the canal, significantly reducing the error of course. Data processing at abnormalities also requires a quality check. The sensor occasionally gives sudden shallow readings in densely watered areas, which are inconsistent with the change in the anterior and posterior dots. The members of the society kept these original points on the map and did not directly delete them, with the markings indicating that the moss is suspected of reflecting water grass moss. They then sampled three locations from the shore using a ribbon gauge, confirming that two of the anomalies did not represent real riverbed swings. This process helps students understand that the data collected automatically still needs to be interpreted in conjunction with environmental and independent checks. At the end of the training, all flight tracks, depth records and operations logs are archived by date. The report specifically distinguishes between lithium sensor readings that have been verified in the field and abnormal values that have not yet been confirmed. The society plans to add a more rigorous repeat route test next time to compare differences in the same route at different wind speeds. This exercise does not seek beautiful 3D graphics, but instead focuses on secure takeover, exception retention and traceable data processing.
