ZH16-01-城市屋顶雨水收集试验 一所职业学院在教学楼屋顶开展了小规模雨水收集试验，目的是让学生观 察降雨、屋面面积和储水量之间的关系。试验系统没有接入生活用水管 网，只为绿化浇灌和设备清洗提供非饮用水。屋顶有效汇水面积约为86平 方米，落水管前端安装了可拆洗滤网，并设置初期弃流装置。2026年6月 18日第一次记录完整降雨过程时，值班学生同时抄录了校内气象站的降雨 量和储水桶液位。 过滤与记录 最初的两次降雨暴露出一个简单问题：滤网上的树叶会明显降低进水速 度。一次12.6毫米的降雨中，理论可收集水量并没有完全进入储水桶，溢 流水反而从屋檐附近排走。维护人员清理滤网后，又在进水口上方加了一 层较粗的拦叶网，使大块杂物在到达细滤网前被截住。修改后的系统在下 一场8.4毫米降雨中运行更稳定，学生也能更清楚地区分“降雨不 足”和“入口受阻”这两种情况。 储水桶外侧标有容量刻度，每次记录都包括起始液位、结束液位、降雨量 和明显的溢流情况。为了避免把蒸发或前一次残留水量误认为本次降雨成 果，课程要求学生按同一时间窗口读取数据。教师还强调，雨量计与屋顶 实际受雨情况并不完全相同，风向、屋面坡度和初期弃流都会造成差异， 因此试验的重点是建立可重复的记录方法，而不是追求某个固定的收集效 率。 一个月后，班级根据数据调整了绿化用水安排。储存的雨水优先用于距离 教学楼最近的花坛，避免用小车长距离搬运。系统旁增加了醒目的“非饮 用水”标识，并规定长期无雨后重新使用前要先检查气味、颜色和容器内 部情况。这个试验规模很小，却让学生看到，简单的节水设施同样需要过 滤、标识、维护和数据记录，才能从演示装置变成可管理的工程系统。

  TRANSLATED: ZH16-01- Urban rooftop rainfall collection experiment A vocational college conducted a small-scale rainfall collection experiment on the roof of a school building to get students to observe the relationship between rainfall, roof area and water storage. The experimental system does not connect to the residential water supply network and provides non-potable water only for green irrigation and equipment cleaning. The roof has an effective drainage area of about 86 square meters, and a removable flush net has been installed at the front of the falling water pipes, and an initial drainage system has been installed. On June 18, 2026, when the first full rainfall was recorded, the students on duty simultaneously transcribed the rainfall and the water level of the water tank stored at the school's weather station. Filtering and recording the first two rains exposed a simple problem: the leaves on the netting significantly slowed the rate of water entry. In a single 12.6 mm rainfall, theoretically the water collected does not enter the reservoir completely, and the overflow drains away from near the roof. After cleaning the netting, the maintenance crew adds a thicker layer of barrier netting above the inlet to stop the large debris before it reaches the net. The modified system operates more steadily in the next 8.4 mm of rainfall, and students are more aware of the two scenarios of insufficient rainfall in the area and blocked entrance of mud. The outer side of the tank is marked with a capacity mark, and each record includes the initial liquid level, the final liquid level, the amount of rainfall and the apparent overflow conditions. In order to avoid mistaking evaporation or previous residual water for the result of this rainfall, the course requires students to read the data in the same time window. The teacher also emphasized that the rainfall measurement is not exactly the same as the actual rainfall on the roof, and that wind direction, roof slope and initial runoff will all make a difference, so the focus of the experiment is to establish a repeatable recording method, rather than pursuing a fixed collection efficiency. A month later, the class adjusted the green water arrangement based on the data. Stored rainwater is preferred for use in flower beds near the school building, avoiding transportation by small trucks. A distinctive non-drinking water fountain logo was added to the system, and it was stipulated that the smell, color and condition of the container should be checked before re-use after a long period of no rain. This small-scale experiment shows students that even a simple water-saving facility needs filtering, labeling, maintenance, and data recording before it can be transformed from a demonstration device into a manageable engineering system.
