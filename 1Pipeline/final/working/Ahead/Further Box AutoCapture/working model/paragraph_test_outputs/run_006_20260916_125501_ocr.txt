ZH12-02-社区图书馆自助归还系统试运行 一家社区图书馆在入口处安装了自助还书设备，希望在闭馆前后的高峰时段减少服务台排队。设备 通过射频标签识别图书，读者把书放入入口槽后，屏幕会显示识别结果并打印简短凭条。图书馆没 有立即取消人工还书窗口，而是安排四周试运行，对识别成功率、误报警、书箱容量和读者操作时 间进行记录。工作人员每天开馆前先检查设备内部是否有卡书或标签未读取的情况。 操作方式的调整 第一周最常见的问题并不是设备故障，而是读者一次放入多本书。系统虽然允许连续处理，但当三 四本书叠在一起进入时，标签位置不同会增加漏读概率。图书馆随后在入口处增加图示，要求一次 放入一本，并把屏幕提示从长句改成两步短指令。第二周的重复操作明显减少。对于没有射频标签 的旧书，设备会明确提示转交人工窗口，而不是把它们当作未知错误。 还书箱的容量也需要重新估算。原设计按平均书厚计算，但儿童绘本和大型画册会更快占满空间。 一次周末活动后，箱体在预计时间前约50分钟达到警戒线。工作人员根据记录调整了周末清空频 率，并在后台加入80%容量提醒。这个提醒只作为工作提示。仍要求工作人员实际查看箱内堆叠情 况，因为书本倾斜可能影响机械测量。 为了检查账户处理是否一致，技术人员每天随机抽取二十笔自助还书记录，与图书管理系统中的借 阅状态进行核对。发现时间戳不一致时，会保留原始日志并检查网络间步，而不是直接修改读者记 录。试运行第三周曾出现一次短暂断网，设备转入离线缓存模式，网络恢复后再同步。工作人员随 后验证了缓存队列中所有记录是否成功写入。四周结束后，自助设备被保留，但人工窗口仍继续开 放。图书馆认为成功的标准不是完全替代工作人员，而是让简单、标准的还书操作更顺畅，同时为 异常情况保留清听的人工处理路径。试运行形成的日志也成为后续维护依据，包括清箱时间，同络 中断、标签读取失放和读者取消操作等信息。

  TRANSLATED: ZH12-02- A community library has installed a self-return device at the entrance of a community library to reduce queues at service desks during peak hours before and after closing. The device identifies the book by means of a radiofrequency tag, and after the reader places the book in the input slot, the screen displays the identification results and prints a short credential. Instead of cancelling the manual return window immediately, the library arranged a four-week trial run to record the success rate of identification, false alarms, bookcase capacity and reader operation time. The staff checks every day before opening the facility for unread cards or labels. The most common problem in the first week of adjusting the operating system is not a device failure, but the reader placing multiple books at once. While the system allows for continuous processing, when three or four books are stacked together, different labeling locations increase the likelihood of misses. The library then added a sign at the entrance, asked to put in one book at a time, and changed the screen prompt from a long sentence to a two-step short instruction. The second week saw a marked decrease in repetition. For older books that do not have RF labels, the device will explicitly prompt to turn over the artificial windows instead of treating them as unknown errors. The capacity of the bookcase also needs to be re-estimated. The original design was based on an average book thickness, but children's drawings and large picture books filled the space faster. After a weekend activity, the tank reached the alert line about 50 minutes before the expected time. Staff adjusted the frequency of the weekend clearance based on the records and added an 80% capacity reminder in the background. This reminder is for work only. Staff are still required to actually inspect the stacking inside the box, as the tilt of the book may affect mechanical measurements. To check for consistency in the accounting process, technicians randomly extract twenty self-refund records per day to check with the borrowing status in the bookkeeping system. When a timestamp is found to be inconsistent, the original logs are kept and network pacing is checked, rather than the reader record being directly modified. During the third week of the trial, there was a brief outage, the device switched to offline caching mode, and the network was restored and synced again. Staff then verified that all records in the cache queue had been successfully written. At the end of the four weeks, the self-help equipment was retained, but the artificial window remained open. The library believes that the criteria for success are not to completely replace the staff, but to make simple, standard book returns work more smoothly, while preserving a clear path for exceptional cases. The logs formed during the trial run also serve as a basis for subsequent maintenance, including information such as check-in times, synchronization interruptions, label read misses, and reader cancellations.
