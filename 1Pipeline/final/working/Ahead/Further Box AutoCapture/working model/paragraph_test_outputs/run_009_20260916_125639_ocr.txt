ZH12-03-海边风机叶片的定期巡检记录 沿海试验场的一台小型风力机需要进行季度巡检。设备长期暴露在盐雾、强风和砂粒环境中，叶片 表面容易出现细小侵蚀。维护团队选择在风速较低的早晨停机检查，并按照锁定程序切断自动启动 功能。地面人员先确认叶轮完全停止，再使用望远镜和高分辨率相机观察三片叶片的前缘、根部和 排水孔。当天环境温度约23℃，平均风速低于4m/s。 与上一次记录相比，第二片叶片靠近尖端的位置出现一条约38毫米长的浅色磨损带。照片显示损 伤仍局限在表面涂层，没有看到明显裂纹。团队用同一拍摄角度和比例尺重新记录该区域，并在维 护图上标出位置。为了避免把光照反射误认为损伤，检查人员又从不同方向拍摄两张照片。三组图 像都能看到相同边界后，该点才被列入跟踪清单。 叶片根部的紧固区域没有发现松动迹象，但一个排水孔周围有盐结晶。技术人员使用规定的软工具 清理表面，没有扩大孔径。机舱外壳和电缆入口也同时检查，因为海边环境中的水汽可能从多个位 置进入。控制柜内的湿度指示片仍处于正常范围，接线端子没有明显变色。维护人员记录了这 些“未发现异常”的项目，避免下一次巡检无法判断某处是否曾经被检查。 恢复运行与复查条件 巡检完成后，风机以低转速恢复运行十五分钟。监控系统中的振动和转速曲线与停机前没有明显差 异。团队没有因此取消对叶尖磨损的后续观察，而是把复查时间设为六周后，并规定如果磨损长度 增长超过10毫米或出现裂纹，就提前安排更详细检查。这样可以在不过度维修的情况下保持明确 的触发条件。最终记录包括停机时间、天气条件、照片编号、检查项目和待办事项。所有照片保留 原始文件，不在归档版本中进行会改变细节的锐化处理。季度巡检的价值不仅在于发现问题，也在 于建立连续证据，使维护人员能够判断一个表面变化是稳定、缓慢发展还是需要立即处理。

  TRANSLATED: A small wind turbine at a coastal test site needs quarterly patrol. Long-term exposure to salt fog, strong winds and sand particles can lead to minor erosion of the surface of the leaf. The maintenance team chooses to check the shutdown in the morning at a low wind speed and disconnect the auto-start function according to the locking procedure. The ground crew first confirms that the leaflet has completely stopped, and then uses telescopes and high-resolution cameras to observe the front edges, roots and drainage holes of the three leaflets. The daytime ambient temperature is about 23°C, with an average wind speed of less than 4 m/s. Compared to the previous record, the second leaf has a light-colored stripe of abrasion 38 mm long near the tip of the leaf. The photos show that the damage is still limited to the surface coating, with no visible cracks. The team re-recorded the area with the same angle and scale and marked the location on the maintenance map. In order to avoid mistaking the light reflection for damage, the inspector took two more photographs from different directions. When all three sets of images see the same boundary, the point is added to the tracking list. The tight areas of the leaf roots show no signs of loosening, but one drainage hole is surrounded by salt crystals. The technician uses the specified soft tools to clean the surface without enlarging the porosity. The cabin shell and cable entrances are also checked at the same time, as water vapour in the seaward environment may enter from multiple locations. The humidity indicator in the control cabinet is still in the normal range, and there is no noticeable discoloration of the wiring terminals. Maintenance personnel recorded the items where no abnormal leaks were found, so that the next inspection would not be able to determine if something had been inspected. After the restoration of operation and the review of conditions, the fan was restored to low speed for fifteen minutes. Vibration and speed curves in the monitoring system did not differ significantly from before the shutdown. The team did not cancel the follow-up observation of the leaf tip wear as a result, but instead set the review time to six weeks later and stipulated that more detailed examinations should be scheduled in advance if the wear length increases by more than 10 mm or if cracks occur. This allows for clear trigger conditions to be maintained without excessive maintenance. The final record includes downtime, weather conditions, photo numbering, check items and pending items. All photos retain their original file and are not subject to incubation which would alter the details in the archived version. The value of a quarterly patrol is not only in discovering problems, but also in establishing continuous evidence that enables maintenance personnel to judge whether a surface change is stable, slow-moving or needs immediate treatment.
