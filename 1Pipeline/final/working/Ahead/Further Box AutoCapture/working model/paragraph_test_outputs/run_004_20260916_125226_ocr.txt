ZH16-03-山区自动气象站的冬季维护 现场检查流程 一座山区自动气象站在冬季前进行年度维护。站点海拔约2140米，主要 记录气温、相对湿度、风速、风向和降水。由于冬季道路可能结冰，维护 人员希望在10月底之前完成传感器检查和备用电源测试。到达现场后，工 作人员先拍摄设备整体状态，再查看立杆、拉线和防雷连接是否松动。所 有拆下的螺钉都放入带盖盒中，避免掉入碎石和积雪区域。 风速传感器是本次检查的重点。过去一个月的记录中，有两段时间出现风 速长时间保持不变的现象。现场转动风杯时能感到轻微阻力，拆开防护罩 后发现内部积有细小砂尘。清洁并重新安装后，风杯转动恢复顺畅。工作 人员没有删除原有异常数据，而是在维护日志中标明检查时间和可能原 因，便于后续分析人员判断哪些时段需要谨慎使用。 电源系统由太阳能板、充电控制器和蓄电池组成。中午测试时，面板表面 部分被薄雪覆盖，清理前后的充电电流差异明显。蓄电池静置电压为12.5 V，接入站点负载后仍保持稳定。维护人员同时检查了防水接头和电缆弯曲 处，因为低温会使老化护套更容易开裂。两个出现硬化迹象的扎带被更 换，并在电缆入口处重新固定应力释放。 离站前，团队让气象站连续运行四十分钟，并将实时数据与手持温湿度仪 和便携式风速仪进行简单对照。这个对照不能代替正式校准，但能发现明 量减少已知风险，并让任何后续异常都有清晰的背景记录。

  TRANSLATED: ZH16-03- Winter maintenance on-site inspection process of a mountain automatic weather station An automatic weather station in the mountains performs annual maintenance before winter. The site is located at an altitude of about 2140 meters above sea level and records mainly temperature, relative humidity, wind speed, wind direction and precipitation. Due to the possibility of freezing roads in winter, maintenance workers hope to complete sensor inspection and backup power testing by the end of October. Upon arrival at the site, the staff takes pictures of the overall condition of the equipment and then checks whether the poles, the tow lines and the mine-proof connections are loose. All the torn screws are placed in a ribbon-covered box to avoid falling into debris and snow areas. Wind speed sensors are the focus of this test. In the last month, there have been two periods of sustained winds in the record. A small resistance can be felt when the windshield is turned on the spot, and when the protective cover is removed, it is found that there is a small accumulation of sand dust inside. After cleaning and reinstalling, the windshield rotates back to normal. Staff did not delete the original data of anomalies, but instead indicated the time of inspection and possible reasons in the maintenance logs, allowing subsequent analysts to determine which times should be used with caution. The power system consists of a solar panel, a charging controller and a battery. During the midday test, the panel surface was partially covered in thin snow, clearing the difference in charging current before and after. The battery has a standby voltage of 12.5 V, which remains stable after the access station load. Maintenance workers also inspected the waterproof connections and cable bends, as the low temperatures make the aging protective covers more likely to crack. Two straps showing signs of hardening are replaced and the stress release is re-fixed at the cable entrance. Before leaving the station, the team had the weather station run continuously for forty minutes, and the real-time data was easily compared with handheld thermometers and portable wind speedometers. This comparison does not replace formal calibration, but it does find a clear reduction in known risk and provides a clear background record for any subsequent abnormalities.
