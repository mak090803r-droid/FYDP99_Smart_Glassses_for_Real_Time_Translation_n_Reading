Offline Inspection at a Remote Energy Facility A maintenance team operates a hybrid-energy station on a dry plateau several hours from the nearest town The station supplies a medical room, a radio repeater, weather instruments, refrigeration, and emergency lighting Solar panels provide most of the daily energy, lithium batteries cover the evening demand, and a compact diesel generator is reserved for long storms. Equipment labels contained model numbers, safety notices, alarm codes. and short repair instructions in several languages The local translation system first checked whether the complete label was visible It rejected frames with a missing edge, heavy glare, severe perspective distortion or poor sharpness in the upper region.
